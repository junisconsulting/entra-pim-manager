# Entra PIM Manager 1.2.0

After you sign in to Windows, the tray now shows your real state without a click, and admins can
lock the tenant list on managed devices.

## New

- **The tray shows your real state right after you sign in to Windows.** The app loads your
  accounts when it starts and signs in silently, so the tray colour and the expiry warning are right
  without opening the panel. If an account needs you to sign in, the Windows sign-in window waits
  until you open the panel.
- **The tray turns red when no account can sign in.** If only some accounts need a new sign-in, the
  tray keeps its usual colour and its tooltip says how many.
- **A lost sign-in comes back by itself when the account is your Windows account.** Some pooled
  virtual desktops lose the app's saved sign-in at every Windows logon. The app now gets a new
  sign-in from Windows silently, but only for an account that is provably the one you signed in to
  Windows with; hybrid and Active Directory sign-ins to Windows are not covered. On Microsoft Entra
  joined hosts with FSLogix, admins fix the cause with the
  `LoadCredKeyFromProfile` policy:
  [Pooled hosts with FSLogix](https://github.com/junisconsulting/entra-pim-manager/blob/main/docs/unattended-deployment.md#pooled-hosts-with-fslogix).
- **The panel can open by itself when the app starts.** Turn on **Open the panel at startup** under
  Settings → Behavior; it is off by default. If a sign-in has expired, the Windows sign-in window
  comes first, then the panel.
- **Admins can lock the tenant list on managed devices.** A machine policy (`TenantLockdown` = 1
  under `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\junis\Entra-PIM-Manager`) removes adding and removing tenants and
  changing their App Registration; labels, ticket systems and accounts stay editable:
  [Lock the tenant list](https://github.com/junisconsulting/entra-pim-manager/blob/main/docs/unattended-deployment.md#lock-the-tenant-list).
- **A script sets up a fresh sign-in for every activation.**
  [`scripts/setup-cap-and-authcontext-for-pim.ps1`](https://github.com/junisconsulting/entra-pim-manager/blob/main/scripts/setup-cap-and-authcontext-for-pim.ps1)
  walks an admin through the steps of the
  [fresh sign-in guide](https://github.com/junisconsulting/entra-pim-manager/blob/main/docs/fresh-sign-in.md)
  and shows every change before it applies it. `-WhatIf` only shows them. It needs PowerShell 7.
- **Settings are shorter.** Explanations now sit behind an ⓘ next to the setting; hover over it to
  read them.

## Fixed

- **An account that needs a new sign-in says what to do.** A missing saved sign-in says so instead
  of asking you to remove the account, and a device-code account says how to sign in again instead
  of pointing to the log.
- **Azure resource roles come back right after a new sign-in.** Before, a missing sign-in kept them
  away for up to an hour.
- **The log no longer repeats the same token cache warning on every refresh.**

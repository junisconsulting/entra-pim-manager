# Entra PIM Manager 0.9.1

A small release about a single icon: the tray icon is visible on a light taskbar again.

## Fixed

- **The tray icon no longer disappears on a light taskbar.** The shield was white with the
  key cut out of it, which reads well against a dark taskbar and against a white one not at
  all — everything but the small red, grey, amber or green status dot vanished, and with it
  the only thing that identifies the icon as Entra PIM Manager. The shield is now a
  mid-tone blue that holds its contrast against a light *and* a dark taskbar, with a light
  outline behind it so the silhouette survives even when the taskbar itself is tinted with
  an accent colour. The four status colours and their meaning are unchanged.

## Notes

- Cosmetic only — nothing about signing in, activating, or the permissions changes.
- Upgrading in place: nothing to re-enter, no new permissions, no admin consent.

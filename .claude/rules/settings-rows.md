A Settings row is a label and its control. Its explanation goes into an ⓘ next to the label
(`<TextBlock Classes="info" ToolTip.Tip="…" />`, style in `Styles/Cards.axaml`), or nowhere if it
only restates the label. Visible text under a row is kept for status, errors, the choices of a
form, and troubleshooting hooks such as the Network check. Decided 2026-11-13.
